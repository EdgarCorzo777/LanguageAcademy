# Schedules, Modalities & Certifications

## 1. Study Schedules and Cohorts

### 1.1 Full-Time Morning Cohort
- **Schedule**: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM (Local Time / UTC-5).
- **Format**: Synchronous live coding sessions, interactive labs, and daily stand-ups.
- **Weekly Commitment**: 25 live instructional hours + 10 self-paced project hours.

### 1.2 Part-Time Evening Cohort
- **Schedule**: Monday through Thursday, 6:00 PM – 9:30 PM, plus Saturday 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (Local Time / UTC-5).
- **Format**: Designed for working professionals, balancing lectures with practical weekend sprints.
- **Weekly Commitment**: 18 live instructional hours + 7 self-paced project hours.

### 1.3 Weekend Intensive Cohort
- **Schedule**: Saturday and Sunday, 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM (Local Time / UTC-5).
- **Format**: Accelerated modular workshops and mentor-guided pair programming.

## 2. Learning Modalities

### 2.1 100% Live Online (Remote)
- Conducted via high-definition video conferencing and interactive cloud-hosted dev environments.
- 1-on-1 mentor office hours available daily.
- Global community access via dedicated Slack/Discord channels.

### 2.2 Hybrid & On-Campus
- Available at our main technological campuses (Medellin, Bogota, and regional hubs).
- Access to high-speed fiber internet, specialized hardware labs, quiet study pods, and coworking spaces.
- Weekly in-person networking events and hiring partner meetups.

## 3. Certifications and Graduation Credentials

### 3.1 Official University Diploma
- **Credential**: Professional Tech Specialist Diploma in the chosen program track.
- **Requirements**:
  - Minimum 85% attendance across all live modules.
  - Completion and passing grade (>= 80%) on all weekly assessments and the final Capstone Project.
  - Successful defense of the final project before the evaluation panel.

### 3.2 Industry Partner Certifications
- **AI Engineering**: Certificate of Competency in LLM Engineering and Python AI Backend. Voucher preparation for AWS Certified Machine Learning / Cloud Practitioner.
- **Full-Stack Web Development**: Full-Stack Software Developer Professional Certificate with verifiable digital badge (Credly-compatible).
- **Cloud & Cybersecurity**: Exam preparation voucher for AWS Solutions Architect Associate or CompTIA Security+.
